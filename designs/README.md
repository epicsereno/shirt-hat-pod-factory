# Design Assets

Drop print-ready artwork here (PNG recommended, transparent background preferred for apparel).

Suggested sizes:
- T-shirts / hoodies: 4500 × 5400 px (or 300 dpi at print size)
- Hats / caps: 1800 × 1800 px or follow the specific product template from your POD partner

Name files to match the `designFile` field in `data/products.json`.
